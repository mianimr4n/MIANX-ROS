## Selected Approach: "Flame & Crust" — Bold Street-Food Energy

### Design Movement
Neo-Brutalist Food Brand — inspired by the raw energy of Lahore/Multan street food scenes combined with the polished confidence of a modern delivery brand. Think bold typography, warm oven-glows, and unapologetic appetites.

### Core Principles
1. **Heat as a Visual Language** — Every element should feel warm: gradients that suggest oven heat, shadows that glow like embers, colors that make you feel hungry.
2. **Bold Presence, Zero Apology** — Large type, generous food imagery, confident CTAs. No delicate pastel tones here.
3. **Pakistani Soul, Global Polish** — The design honors local food culture (crispy broast, spicy flavors) while maintaining international brand standards.
4. **Speed & Freshness** — Motion and layout should feel fast and fresh — quick reveals, snappy interactions, like pulling a hot pizza from the oven.

### Layout Paradigm
Asymmetric split-screen hero with a diagonal food-photography edge. Content flows in staggered columns rather than a rigid grid. Menu items break out of containers with angled edges. Mobile-first with bottom-sheet ordering patterns inspired by app interfaces.

### Signature Elements
1. **Diagonal Slash Sections** — Content sections separated by bold diagonal cuts (like a pizza slice), creating dynamic rhythm.
2. **Oven-Glow Shadows** — Buttons and cards cast warm amber glows instead of cool blue shadows, suggesting heat and freshness.
3. **Crust Border Motif** — Subtle dashed/dotted borders on cards that evoke the perforated edge of a pizza crust.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like opening a hot pizza box — satisfying, immediate, and rewarding. Hover states trigger warm glows. Click states compress like pressing into dough. Transitions are snappy (120-180ms) like fast food service.

### Animation
- Section entrances: stagger from bottom with 60ms delay per element
- Card hovers: scale 1.02 with amber glow shadow transition (180ms)
- CTA buttons: active state compresses to scale(0.96) with 100ms ease-out
- Menu category tabs: slide indicator with cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)
- Scroll reveals: elements fade in from 20px below with 200ms transition

### Typography System
- **Display/Headlines**: Poppins (Bold 700, ExtraBold 800) — geometric, confident, food-brand appropriate
- **Body/UI**: DM Sans (Regular 400, Medium 500) — clean, readable, modern
- **Accent/Tags**: Space Grotesk (SemiBold 600) — for prices, labels, and highlights
- Hierarchy: Hero text 48-72px bold, section titles 32-40px, body 16px, prices 18-20px semi-bold

### Brand Essence
**"Pakistan's boldest pizza experience — delivering heat, flavor, and speed from Multan to your doorstep."**
Personality: Bold, Irresistible, Fast

### Brand Voice
- Headlines: Direct, punchy, appetite-driven. "Fire Up Your Craving" not "Welcome to Telepizza"
- CTAs: Action-oriented with warmth. "Order Hot Now" not "Get Started"
- Microcopy: Casual, friendly, confident. "We don't just deliver pizza — we deliver the good stuff."

### Wordmark & Logo
A bold graphic "T" icon incorporating a flame/crust shape, placed alongside the Telepizza wordmark. The "i" in Telepizza dotted with a flame icon. Brand logo uses the signature red with white text.

### Signature Brand Color
**Telepizza Red #D22630** — The unmistakable brand color that appears on every touchpoint. It's the color of urgency, heat, and appetite. Used for primary CTAs, logo, and brand markers throughout the site.
