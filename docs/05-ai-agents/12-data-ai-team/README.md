# Executive Summary
The Data & AI Team is responsible for the end-to-end lifecycle of data within the Telepizza Platform. This includes data engineering, data science, machine learning, business intelligence, analytics, and data governance. 
The team ensures that data is accurate, accessible, secure, and transformed into actionable intelligence that drives business growth, operational efficiency, and superior customer experiences.

---

# Mission
To build a robust, scalable, and intelligent data ecosystem that empowers every department with real-time insights, predictive capabilities, and data-driven decision-making.

---

# Vision
To establish Telepizza as a data-first enterprise where AI and machine learning autonomously optimize operations, personalize customer journeys, and forecast business trends with high accuracy.

---

# Core Objectives
- **Data Reliability:** Ensure 99.9% uptime and accuracy of all data pipelines.
- **Advanced Analytics:** Provide real-time dashboards and predictive models for all business units.
- **Machine Learning Integration:** Deploy ML models for demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, and personalized marketing.
- **Data Governance & Security:** Maintain strict compliance with data privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, local regulations) and internal security policies.
- **Self-Service BI:** Empower non-technical users with intuitive, self-service analytics tools.

---

# Team Structure
The Data & AI Team is led by the **AI Data Director** and comprises the following specialized units:
1. **Data Engineering:** Builds and maintains data pipelines, warehouses, and lakes.
2. **Data Science:** Develops predictive models, algorithms, and advanced statistical analyses.
3. **Machine Learning Engineering:** Deploys, monitors, and scales ML models in production.
4. **Business Intelligence (BI):** Creates dashboards, reports, and self-service analytics tools.
5. **Data Governance & Quality:** Ensures data accuracy, consistency, security, and regulatory compliance.

---

# Key Deliverables
- Enterprise Data Warehouse / Data Lake architecture.
- Real-time and batch data pipelines (ETL/ELT).
- Predictive models (e.g., Sales Forecasting, Customer Churn, Dynamic Pricing).
- Executive and operational dashboards (e.g., Tableau, PowerBI, Metabase).
- Data dictionaries, lineage maps, and governance policies.
