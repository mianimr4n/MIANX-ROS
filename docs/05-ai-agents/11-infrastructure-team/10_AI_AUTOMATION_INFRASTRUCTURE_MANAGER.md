# Executive Summary
The AI Automation Infrastructure Manager is responsible for designing, implementing and maintaining the automation infrastructure that enables autonomous operations across the Telepizza Platform.
This role ensures that infrastructure provisioning, configuration, deployment, monitoring and recovery are automated, reducing manual intervention and improving operational efficiency.
The AI Automation Infrastructure Manager owns the complete automation infrastructure lifecycle from design to continuous improvement.
---
# Mission
Deliver a fully automated infrastructure platform capable of provisioning, configuring, deploying, monitoring and recovering itself with minimal human intervention.
---
# Vision
Create an AI-driven automation platform where infrastructure is self-healing, self-optimizing and autonomously managed.
