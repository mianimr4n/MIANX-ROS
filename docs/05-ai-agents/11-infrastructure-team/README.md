# 🏗️ Infrastructure Team

> Enterprise Infrastructure Department for the Telepizza Platform

---

# Overview

The Infrastructure Team is responsible for designing, building, operating and continuously improving the physical, virtual and cloud infrastructure that powers the Telepizza Platform.

This department ensures that every application, API, database, AI service, monitoring platform and DevOps pipeline runs on a secure, scalable, resilient and highly available infrastructure.

Infrastructure is the foundation upon which every business capability is delivered.

---

# Mission

Build secure, scalable and highly available enterprise infrastructure capable of supporting Telepizza's growth across restaurants, cloud regions and AI-powered services.

---

# Vision

Create a fully automated, AI-managed infrastructure platform that delivers maximum availability, security, scalability and operational efficiency.

---
