# Management Structure

## Executive Level

- Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Responsible for:

- Technology Vision
- Enterprise Architecture
- Strategic Investment
- Business Technology Alignment

---

## Department Level

### AI Infrastructure Director

Responsible for:

- Department Leadership
- Infrastructure Strategy
- Governance
- Standards
- Budget
- Executive Reporting

---

## Management Level

### AI Network Infrastructure Manager

Owns

- Enterprise Networking
- Firewalls
- Routing
- Switching
- VPN
- Internet Connectivity

---

### AI Server Infrastructure Manager

Owns

- Physical Servers
- Linux
- Windows Servers
- Compute Infrastructure
- OS Lifecycle

---

### AI Storage Infrastructure Manager

Owns

- SAN
- NAS
- Object Storage
- Backup Storage
- Storage Performance

---

### AI Virtualization Manager

- VMware
- Proxmox
- Hyper-V
- Virtual Machines
- Cluster Management

---

### AI Platform Infrastructure Manager

Owns

- Compute Platform

- Core Services

### AI Capacity Planning Manager
