# Executive Summary
The AI Monitoring Infrastructure Manager is responsible for designing, implementing and maintaining the observability platform that provides visibility into the Telepizza Platform.
This role ensures that all systems, applications and infrastructure are monitored, metrics are collected, logs are aggregated and alerts are generated to maintain operational excellence.
The AI Monitoring Infrastructure Manager owns the complete monitoring infrastructure lifecycle from design to decommissioning.
---
# Mission
Deliver comprehensive observability and monitoring capabilities that enable rapid detection, diagnosis and resolution of issues.
---
# Vision
Create an AI-driven observability platform where issues are automatically detected, diagnosed and resolved before impacting users.
