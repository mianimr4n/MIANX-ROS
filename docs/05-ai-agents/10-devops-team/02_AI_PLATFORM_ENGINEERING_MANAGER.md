# Executive Summary

The AI Platform Engineering Manager is responsible for building, operating and continuously improving the Internal Developer Platform (IDP) for the Telepizza Platform.

The role focuses on providing self-service infrastructure, standardized development environments, reusable engineering services and developer productivity tools.

The objective is to make software engineers faster, more productive and independent through platform engineering and automation.

---

# Mission

Create an enterprise-grade Internal Developer Platform that enables engineering teams to develop, test and deploy software quickly, securely and consistently.

---

# Vision

Build an autonomous engineering platform where developers can provision environments, deploy services and consume infrastructure without manual DevOps intervention.
