# Purpose

This document defines the official organization structure, reporting hierarchy, decision authority and collaboration model of the DevOps Department.

It establishes how every AI DevOps Employee works together to automate software delivery, operate cloud infrastructure, manage production environments and maintain platform reliability across the Telepizza Platform and future Mianx.ai ecosystem.
