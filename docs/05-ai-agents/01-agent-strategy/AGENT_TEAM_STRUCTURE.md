# Purpose

This document defines the organizational structure of the Telepizza AI Workforce.

It establishes the hierarchy, reporting relationships, responsibilities, collaboration model, and governance framework for all AI employees operating within the Telepizza Platform.

---

# Objectives

- Establish a structured AI organization
- Define clear reporting lines
- Separate business and engineering responsibilities
- Enable multi-agent collaboration
- Ensure governance and accountability
- Support scalable AI workforce growth

---

# AI Organization Principles

Every AI team shall be

- Specialized
- Collaborative
- Governed
- Secure
- Observable
- Auditable
- Human Supervised
