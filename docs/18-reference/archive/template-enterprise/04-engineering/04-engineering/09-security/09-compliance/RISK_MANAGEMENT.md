# 1. Purpose

This document defines the enterprise framework for identifying, assessing, treating, monitoring, and reporting business, cybersecurity, operational, infrastructure, cloud, and AI risks across the Telepizza Platform.

Risk management is a continuous governance process that supports informed decision-making.

---

# 2. Vision

Enterprise risk management shall be

- Continuous
- Data Driven
- AI Assisted
- Business Aligned
- Measurable
- Auditable

Every significant business decision should consider risk.

---

# 3. Objectives

The Risk Management Framework provides

- Enterprise Risk Governance
- Cyber Risk Assessment
- AI Risk Management
- Risk Prioritization
- Risk Treatment
- Continuous Monitoring
- Executive Reporting

# 5. Risk Categories

Enterprise risks include

- Strategic Risk
- Operational Risk
- Cybersecurity Risk
- Cloud Risk
- Infrastructure Risk
- AI Risk
- Privacy Risk
- Compliance Risk
- Financial Risk
- Third-Party Risk

Every identified risk shall have an assigned owner.

---

# 6. Risk Assessment

Each risk defines

- Risk ID
- Description
- Business Impact
- Likelihood
- Severity
- Overall Risk Rating
- Owner
- Review Date

Risk assessments should follow a standardized methodology.

---

# 7. Risk Matrix

| Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level |
|------------|--------|------------|
| Low | Low | Low |
| Medium | Medium | Medium |
| High | High | High |
| Critical | Critical | Critical |

Critical risks require executive visibility and treatment plans.

---

# 8. Risk Treatment

Treatment options include

- Avoid
- Reduce
- Transfer
- Accept

Every accepted risk requires documented business approval.

---

# 9. AI Risk Management

Evaluate

- Prompt Injection
- Hallucinations
- Model Bias
- Unsafe Tool Usage
- Data Leakage
- Autonomous Decisions
- Model Supply Chain Risk

High-risk AI operations require additional controls.

---

# 10. Continuous Monitoring

Monitor

- Emerging Threats
- Security Events
- Compliance Drift
- Infrastructure Changes
- AI Behavior
- Vulnerability Trends

Risk scores should be updated when significant changes occur.

---

# 11. Risk Reporting

Reports include

- Executive Dashboard
- High-Risk Register
- Treatment Status
- Risk Trends
- Open Issues
- Residual Risk

Reports should support both executives and technical teams.

---

# 12. Governance

Every risk defines

- Business Owner
- Technical Owner
- Treatment Plan
- Target Resolution Date
- Approval Authority
- Review Frequency

Risk reviews should occur at least quarterly.

---

# 13. Enterprise KPIs

| KPI | Target |
|------|---------|
| Risk Register Coverage | 100% |
| Critical Risk Treatment | ≥95% |
| Risk Review Completion | 100% |
| AI Risk Assessment Coverage | 100% |
| Residual Risk Visibility | 100% |

---

# 14. Best Practices

- Identify risks early.
- Maintain a centralized risk register.
- Prioritize risks consistently.
- Review treatment effectiveness regularly.
- Continuously monitor residual risk.
- Integrate AI risk into enterprise governance.
