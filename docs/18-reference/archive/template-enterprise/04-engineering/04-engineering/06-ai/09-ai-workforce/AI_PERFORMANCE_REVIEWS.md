# 1. Purpose

This document defines how AI employees are periodically evaluated to ensure they continue delivering high-quality, secure, reliable, and cost-effective outcomes.

Performance reviews identify strengths, weaknesses, improvement opportunities, and lifecycle decisions.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Performance Review Framework provides

- Continuous Improvement
- Quality Assurance
- Workforce Optimization
- Governance Compliance
- Promotion Readiness
- Retraining Decisions
- Retirement Decisions

---

# 3. Review Lifecycle

```
Performance Data

↓

KPI Analysis

↓

↓

↓

↓

Decision

↓

↓

Follow-up Review
```

# 8. Review Outcomes

Possible outcomes

- Continue Current Role
- Promotion
- Capability Upgrade
- Additional Training
- Prompt Optimization
- Model Change
- Workflow Reassignment
- Temporary Restrictions
- Retirement

---

# 9. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

If performance is below target

```
Performance Issue

↓

Root Cause Analysis

↓

Improvement Plan

↓

Implementation

↓

Re-Evaluation

↓

Success / Escalation
```

Improvement plans should include measurable objectives and review dates.

---

# 10. Promotion Criteria

Promotion may be considered when an AI employee demonstrates

- Consistent KPI Achievement
- High Reliability
- Strong Collaboration
- Policy Compliance
- Low Error Rate
- Positive Human Feedback

Promotions require governance approval.

---

# 11. Capability Upgrades

Upgrades may include

- New Tools
- Additional Permissions
- Larger Context Window
- Better Model Assignment
- Expanded Workflow Participation
- New Department Responsibilities

All upgrades must pass security and governance reviews.

---

# 12. Human Feedback

Collect feedback from

- Workflow Owners
- Department Leaders
- Business Users
- Technical Reviewers
- Platform Administrators

Feedback should influence future improvements.

---

# 13. Retraining Triggers

Retraining is recommended when

- Error Rate Increases
- KPI Declines
- New Business Rules Are Introduced
- Knowledge Base Changes Significantly
- New Models Become Available

---

# 14. Review Dashboard

Track

- Overall Performance Score
- Trend Analysis
- KPI Achievement
- Department Ranking
- Promotion Candidates
- Improvement Plans

---

# 15. Historical Records

Maintain

- Review History
- KPI History
- Promotions
- Capability Changes
- Incidents
- Improvement Plans

Historical records must remain immutable.

---

# 16. Governance

Performance reviews must follow

- AI Constitution
- AI Governance Framework
- Security Policies
- Human Approval Workflows

No review may bypass governance requirements.

---

# 17. Best Practices

- Base reviews on objective data.
- Balance quality, speed, and cost.
- Encourage continuous improvement.
- Reward sustained excellence.
- Act quickly on declining performance.
- Preserve complete review history.
