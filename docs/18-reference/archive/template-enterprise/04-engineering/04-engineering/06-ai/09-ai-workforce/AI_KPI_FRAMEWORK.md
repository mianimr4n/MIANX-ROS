# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Key Performance Indicator (KPI) framework for measuring the effectiveness, efficiency, quality, reliability, and business impact of AI employees.

Every AI employee must have measurable objectives aligned with organizational goals.

- Continuous Improvement

# 19. Best Practices

- Measure outcomes, not activity.
- Align KPIs with business goals.
- Review KPIs regularly.
- Keep metrics transparent.
- Balance quality, speed, and cost.
- Avoid optimizing a single metric at the expense of others.
