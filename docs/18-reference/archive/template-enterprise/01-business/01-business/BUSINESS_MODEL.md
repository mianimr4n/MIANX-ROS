# Executive Summary

The Telepizza Platform is designed to support a scalable, technology-driven restaurant business. The goal is to increase customer satisfaction, improve operational efficiency, and enable multi-branch growth through a centralized digital platform.

# Revenue Streams

**🟢 Confirmed real (current menu categories, verified):**

- Pizza Sales (including signature Kabab Stuffed Crust)
- Burgers/Sandwiches
- Fries & sides
- Drinks, shakes & frappés (a genuine strength per real reviews)

**🟡 Plausible near-term (not yet confirmed as active):**

- Delivery Charges — model not yet decided, see `DELIVERY_POLICY.md`
- Combo Deals — at least one real bundle observed (~Rs 1,899), full combo lineup unconfirmed
- Corporate Orders — no evidence yet this demand exists; validate before building for it

**🔴 Speculative / long-term only (no current basis, don't plan technical work around these yet):**

- Catering
- Franchise Fees — see flag in `FRANCHISE_MODEL.md`, franchise intent itself is unconfirmed
- Merchandise
- Gift Cards
- Subscription Offers
