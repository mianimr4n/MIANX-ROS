# Executive Summary

Telepizza Platform is an integrated digital ecosystem developed to manage every aspect of Telepizza Pakistan.

The platform combines customer ordering, restaurant operations, delivery management, business analytics, marketing automation, and AI-powered assistance into one centralized system.

Initially, the platform will support the following branches:

- Royal Orchard Branch
- Northern Bypass Road Branch

The architecture is designed to support unlimited future branches without requiring major system changes.
