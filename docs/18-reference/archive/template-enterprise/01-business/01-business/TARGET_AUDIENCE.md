# Overview

The Telepizza Platform is designed to serve a diverse range of customers with different needs, ordering habits, and purchasing behavior.

Understanding these customer groups helps improve marketing, product offerings, customer experience, and AI-driven personalization.

# Customer Personas

⚠️ **These are illustrative/hypothetical personas, not real customer research.** No actual Telepizza customer data (names, ages, ordering frequency) was collected or exists yet in this project. They're useful as thinking tools for design discussions, but should not be cited as findings or shown to Telepizza as "customer research" — that would misrepresent invented examples as real data.

## Illustrative Persona 1 – "The Student" (hypothetical)

- Age range: college/university-aged (illustrative only)
- Assumed to value budget pricing and late-hour availability
- **Real version of this persona should be built from actual order data once online ordering launches**

---

## Illustrative Persona 2 – "The Family Orderer" (hypothetical)

- Assumed to order for multiple people, values reliability over speed
- **Real version needs confirmation — e.g., via a simple question at checkout or informal conversation with regular walk-in customers**

---

## Illustrative Persona 3 – "The Office/Corporate Orderer" (hypothetical)

- Assumed interest in larger orders, invoicing
- **No evidence yet this segment exists for Telepizza specifically — corporate demand should be validated before building corporate-specific features (invoicing, credit terms), not assumed**

## What We Actually Know (From Real Review Data)

See `customer-journey.md` for verified findings: real reviewers mention 3-year loyalty, praise for beverages/desserts, and specific complaints about delivery time and food temperature. This is thin compared to full personas, but it's real — unlike the personas above, which are placeholders until real data exists.

# Customer Pain Points

**Confirmed real pain points** (from actual Telepizza reviews — see `customer-journey.md`):

- Delivery time inconsistency (one reviewer: ~1hr20min)
- Food arriving cold/dry
- Missing condiments (sauce/ketchup)
- No order status visibility between placing the call and food arriving

**Additional plausible risks** (not yet confirmed, listed for planning purposes only):

- Limited payment options
- Out-of-stock items not communicated

The platform should minimize these through automation and operational excellence — but the confirmed list above should drive Phase 3 priorities, since it's evidence-based rather than assumed.
