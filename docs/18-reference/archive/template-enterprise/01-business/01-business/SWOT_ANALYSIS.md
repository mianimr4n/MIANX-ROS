# Threats

## Strong Competition

The most concrete competitive threat is **Johnnyboy's Pizza**, literally the same block in Royal Orchard, currently rated higher (4.5★ vs Telepizza's 4.3★) — though their recent reviews show growing consistency complaints (see `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`). This is a real, immediate, local threat — not a generic "competitors exist" statement.

Mitigation

- Better digital experience
- Faster delivery
- AI-powered operations
- Specifically: winning on order-consistency, since that's where both Telepizza and Johnnyboy's are currently weak per real reviews
