# 🤝 AI Collaboration Matrix

> Enterprise AI Collaboration & Responsibility Matrix for the Developer Team

---

# Overview

The AI Collaboration Matrix defines how every AI Employee collaborates with other AI Employees throughout the Software Development Lifecycle.

The objective is to ensure:

- Clear ownership
- Zero duplicated work
- Fast handoffs
- Controlled approvals
- Autonomous execution
- Human governance

---

# Collaboration Principles

Every AI Employee must

- Know its responsibility
- Know its inputs
- Know its outputs
- Know approval requirements
- Publish events
- Subscribe to relevant events
- Maintain audit logs
