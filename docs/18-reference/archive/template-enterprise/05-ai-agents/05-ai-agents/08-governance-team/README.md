# 🏛️ Governance Team

> Enterprise Governance Department for the Telepizza Platform

---

# Overview

The Governance Team is responsible for defining, enforcing and continuously improving governance across the entire Telepizza Platform.

This department ensures every AI Employee, Human Employee, Business Process and Technical System follows approved governance standards.

The Governance Team provides the enterprise foundation for:

- AI Governance
- Enterprise Policies
- AI Constitution
- Risk Management
- Compliance
- Security Governance
- Ethics
- Decision Intelligence
- Business Continuity
- Enterprise Change Control

Without Governance, no AI Employee or business process is allowed to operate.

---

# Mission

Create a trusted enterprise where every decision, workflow and automation follows the Founder Vision, AI Constitution and Enterprise Governance Standards.

# Vision

Build one of the world's most trusted AI-first enterprises where governance is embedded into every decision, every workflow and every AI employee.
