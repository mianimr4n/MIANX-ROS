# Purpose

This document defines the official governance operating model for the Telepizza Platform.

It provides standards, principles, decision rules, governance lifecycle, review processes and best practices for every AI Employee and human team member.

---

# Governance Mission

Ensure every business decision, AI action, software release and operational process follows approved governance standards.

---

# Governance Vision

Build a secure, transparent, accountable and AI-first enterprise where governance is embedded into every workflow.

---

# Governance Principles

## 1. Founder First

Founder Vision is always the highest authority.

---

## 2. Constitution First

Every AI Employee must follow the AI Constitution.

---

## 3. Policy Before Action

No business activity starts without an approved policy.

---

## 4. Security by Default

Every workflow must be secure from the beginning.

---

## 5. Zero Trust

Never trust.
Always verify.

---

## 6. Human Oversight

Critical decisions always support human review and approval.

---

## 7. Explainable AI

Every important AI decision must be understandable and auditable.

---

## 8. Full Traceability

Every important action must leave an audit trail.

---

## 9. Continuous Improvement

Governance is continuously reviewed and improved.

# Governance Decision Rules

Every decision must answer:

- Is it constitutional?
- Is it compliant?
- Is it secure?
- Is it ethical?
- Is the risk acceptable?
- Is approval required?
- Can it be audited?
- Does it support business objectives?

If any answer is **No**, the decision must be escalated.

- Purpose

- Owner
- Responsibilities

- Dependencies
- Approval Rules
- Revision History

# AI Employee Standards

Every AI Employee must:

- Follow Founder Vision
- Follow AI Constitution
- Follow Enterprise Policies
- Record every important action
- Escalate high-risk decisions
- Protect confidential data
- Maintain complete audit trails

---

# Security Standards

Every AI Employee shall:

- Apply least privilege
- Follow Zero Trust
- Validate permissions
- Encrypt sensitive data
- Log every critical event
- Report security incidents immediately

---

# Compliance Standards

Every workflow must satisfy:

- Enterprise Policies
- AI Constitution
- Internal Controls
- Security Standards
- Audit Standards
- Regulatory Requirements

---

# Risk Standards

Every project must include:

- Risk Identification
- Risk Classification
- Risk Owner
- Mitigation Plan
- Monitoring Plan
- Review Schedule

---

# Audit Standards

Every activity must generate:

- Timestamp
- Actor
- Action
- Reason
- Outcome
- Approval Record
- Trace ID

Audit records must never be modified or deleted.

---

# Approval Standards

Approval depends on:

- Business Risk
- Financial Impact
- Customer Impact
- Security Impact
- Compliance Impact
- Strategic Importance

---

# Incident Standards

Every incident requires:

- Classification
- Severity
- Owner
- Timeline
- Recovery Plan
- Root Cause Analysis
- Lessons Learned

---

# Change Management Standards

Every change requires:

- RFC

- Risk Review
- Rollback Plan

# Business Continuity Standards

# Governance Best Practices

- Keep governance simple.
- Automate repetitive reviews.
- Minimize manual approvals.
- Measure everything.
- Continuously improve.
- Prioritize business value.
- Document every important decision.
- Protect customer trust.
