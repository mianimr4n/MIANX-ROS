# Executive Summary

The AI DevOps Director is the executive leader responsible for defining, governing and continuously improving the DevOps strategy across the Telepizza Platform.

The role ensures software delivery is fast, secure, reliable, scalable and fully automated while aligning DevOps initiatives with the overall technology strategy of Mianx.ai.

---

# Mission

Create an enterprise-class DevOps organization that delivers software continuously through automation, platform engineering and operational excellence.

---

# Vision

Build a fully autonomous AI DevOps organization capable of deploying, operating and optimizing software with minimal human intervention.
