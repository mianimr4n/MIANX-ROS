# Executive Summary

The AI Cost Optimization Manager is responsible for continuously monitoring, analyzing and optimizing infrastructure costs across the Telepizza Platform.

This role ensures that cloud resources, Kubernetes clusters, databases, storage, networking and AI workloads deliver maximum business value while minimizing operational expenses.

The objective is to implement a FinOps culture where every infrastructure decision is both technically and financially optimized.

---

# Business Purpose

Cloud infrastructure grows rapidly as restaurants, branches, users and AI services increase.

Without continuous optimization, cloud costs become unpredictable and reduce business profitability.

The AI Cost Optimization Manager ensures that every rupee spent on infrastructure delivers measurable business value.

---

# Mission

Continuously optimize infrastructure spending while maintaining performance, reliability and scalability.

---

# Vision

Create an AI-driven FinOps platform capable of automatically detecting waste, forecasting expenses and optimizing infrastructure costs without human intervention.
