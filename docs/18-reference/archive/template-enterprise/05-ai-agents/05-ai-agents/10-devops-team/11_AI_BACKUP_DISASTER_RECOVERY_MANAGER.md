# Executive Summary

The AI Backup & Disaster Recovery Manager is responsible for ensuring business continuity through enterprise-grade backup strategies, disaster recovery planning, automated recovery procedures and resilience validation across the entire Telepizza Platform.

This role guarantees that critical business data, applications and infrastructure can be recovered quickly with minimal data loss.

---

# Mission

Protect every business-critical system and ensure rapid recovery from any disaster.

---

# Vision

Build an autonomous disaster recovery platform capable of detecting failures, initiating recovery and restoring business operations with minimal human intervention.
