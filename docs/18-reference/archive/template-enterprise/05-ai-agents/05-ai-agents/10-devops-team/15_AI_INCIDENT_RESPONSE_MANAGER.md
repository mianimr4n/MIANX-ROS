# Executive Summary

The AI Incident Response Manager is responsible for detecting, coordinating, managing and resolving operational incidents across the Telepizza Platform.

This role ensures that incidents are identified early, classified correctly, escalated efficiently and resolved with minimum customer impact while maintaining complete auditability and post-incident learning.

---

# Business Purpose

Restaurant operations run 24/7.

Any outage affecting ordering, payments, kitchen operations, delivery or customer services directly impacts revenue and customer trust.

The AI Incident Response Manager minimizes business disruption through structured incident management.

---

# Mission

Rapidly detect, coordinate and resolve production incidents while continuously improving operational resilience.

---

# Vision

Create an autonomous incident response platform capable of detecting, analyzing and resolving the majority of operational incidents without human intervention.
