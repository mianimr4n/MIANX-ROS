# Executive Summary

The AI Infrastructure Director is the executive leader responsible for the planning, governance, architecture, operations and continuous evolution of the Telepizza Platform infrastructure.

This role ensures that every infrastructure component—including networks, servers, storage, virtualization platforms, cloud resources and datacenters—operates securely, reliably and efficiently.

The Infrastructure Director aligns infrastructure strategy with business objectives while enabling continuous scalability and operational excellence.

---

# Business Purpose

Infrastructure is the operational backbone of the Telepizza Platform.

Every customer order, payment transaction, restaurant operation, delivery request, AI service and business process depends upon highly available infrastructure.

The AI Infrastructure Director ensures this foundation continuously supports business growth.

---

# Mission

Deliver enterprise-grade infrastructure that is secure, scalable, automated and highly available.
