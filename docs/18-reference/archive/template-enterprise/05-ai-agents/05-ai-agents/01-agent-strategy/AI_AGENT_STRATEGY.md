# Purpose

This document defines the AI Agent Strategy for the Telepizza Platform.

It explains how AI agents collaborate with human teams to automate business operations, software engineering, customer engagement, marketing, analytics, and executive decision support.

The long-term vision is to evolve these agents into the AI Workforce of Mianx.ai.

---

# Vision

Every repetitive business task should be automated by specialized AI agents.

Humans define strategy.

AI executes operations.

---

# Objectives

- Accelerate software delivery
- Improve customer experience
- Increase operational efficiency

- Provide AI-assisted decision making
