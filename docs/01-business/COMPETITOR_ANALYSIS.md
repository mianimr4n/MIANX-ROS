# Primary Competitors (Verified, Multan-Specific)

## Johnnyboy's Pizza — Direct Competitor

**Same block as Telepizza:** E Block, Street 7, Royal Orchard, Multan.

Strengths

- 4.5★ on foodpanda (79 reviews)
- Also markets an "injected chicken" pizza — direct product overlap with Telepizza's "Injected Broast"
- Delivery, takeout, and dine-in available

Weaknesses (from real recent reviews, late 2025–2026)

- Recurring complaints of inconsistent quality and reduced ingredients/cheese over time
- Multiple repeat-customer complaints of "burnt food" and feeling overpriced
- A pattern of declining satisfaction from at least one heavy repeat reviewer

Opportunity for Telepizza

Johnnyboy's is losing consistency with regulars — the same failure mode Telepizza's own reviews show (cold food, missing items). Whichever business fixes order-visibility and consistency first has a real, current local edge — this isn't hypothetical, it's visible in live reviews right now.

---

## The Melt 9 Pizza & Steak House

Strengths

- Very high review volume (3,115 Google reviews) — strong market reach
- Broader menu (pizza + steak house)

Weaknesses

- Lower rating (3.9★) than both Telepizza (4.3★) and Johnnyboy's (4.5★) despite the volume

Opportunity for Telepizza

Higher rating at smaller scale suggests room to compete on quality/consistency rather than trying to match Melt 9's volume/marketing spend directly.

---

## Texas Pizza

Strengths

- 4.3★ rating, comparable to Telepizza

Weaknesses

- Small review base (24 reviews) — limited market presence to judge by

---

## Citywide Context (Not Competitor-Specific)

- Several Multan pizza/fast-food competitors (Johnnyboy's, Second Street Pizza, EPIC, Amfahh, Meat N Eat) are already listed on **foodpanda** with delivery infrastructure and visible ratings.
- Telepizza's own foodpanda presence was not found in this research. If Telepizza isn't listed there, that's a real distribution gap — customers browsing foodpanda for pizza in Multan won't see Telepizza at all, regardless of food quality. Worth a direct decision in Phase 3: is an aggregator presence part of this platform's scope?
- KFC has a Multan branch and functions as the "default choice" many local fast-food customers compare everything else against.

**What was removed:** Pizza Max, Broadway Pizza, Domino's Pakistan, and Pizza Online from the earlier draft — kept out because none were verified as operating in or near Royal Orchard/Northern Bypass Road specifically. If any of these do have a relevant nearby branch, that should be confirmed directly rather than assumed.
