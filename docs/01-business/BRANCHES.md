# Overview

Telepizza Pakistan operates through multiple branches managed from one centralized digital platform.

Each branch has its own staff, inventory, kitchen, riders, and sales data, while Head Office maintains complete visibility and control.

# Delivery Coverage

Every branch has its own delivery zones.

The system automatically assigns customer orders to the nearest available branch based on:

- Customer Location
- Delivery Radius
- Branch Availability
