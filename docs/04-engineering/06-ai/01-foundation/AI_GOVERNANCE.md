# 1. Purpose

This document defines the governance framework for all AI systems within the Telepizza Platform.

It establishes decision authority, operational boundaries, approval requirements, accountability, and compliance expectations for AI-assisted business operations.

---

# 2. Governance Objectives

The AI Governance Framework ensures

- Responsible AI
- Human Oversight
- Enterprise Compliance
- Risk Management
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Auditability
- Continuous Improvement

---

# 3. Governance Principles

Every AI capability must follow

- Human Accountability
- Transparency
- Fairness
- Security
- Privacy
- Explainability
- Traceability
- Least Privilege

# 5. AI Decision Levels

Level 1

Informational

Examples

- FAQ
- Knowledge Search
- Summaries

Human Approval

Not Required

---

Level 2

Operational Assistance

Examples

- Reports
- Recommendations
- Planning

Human Approval

Optional

---

Level 3

Business Operations

Examples

- Inventory Updates
- Scheduling
- Notifications

Human Approval

Policy Driven

---

Level 4

High Risk Operations

Examples

- Refunds
- Price Changes
- User Management
- Financial Adjustments

Human Approval

Required

---

Level 5

Critical Enterprise Actions

Examples

- Security Policies
- Organization Configuration
- Data Deletion
- Financial Approvals

Human Approval

Mandatory

---

# 6. Risk Classification

Low

Medium

High

Critical

Every workflow must declare its risk level.

---

# 7. Human-in-the-Loop

Human approval is required for

- Financial Transactions
- Refunds
- Employee Access
- Role Changes
- Sensitive Customer Data
- Security Configuration
- Legal Decisions

The AI may prepare recommendations but must wait for approval before execution.

---

# 8. AI Responsibilities

AI may

- Analyze
- Recommend
- Summarize
- Classify
- Draft
- Automate Approved Tasks

AI must not

- Override governance
- Bypass security
- Escalate privileges
- Ignore approval requirements

---

# 9. Policy Engine

Policies define

- Allowed Actions
- Restricted Actions
- Approval Thresholds
- Tool Permissions
- Data Access Rules

Policies should be centrally managed and version controlled.

---

# 10. Agent Governance

Every AI agent must define

- Owner
- Purpose
- Permissions
- Allowed Tools
- Approval Requirements
- Risk Classification

# 13. Audit Requirements

Record

- Decision ID
- Workflow ID
- Agent ID
- User ID
- Risk Level
- Approval Status
- Timestamp
- Outcome

Audit records should be immutable.

---

# 14. Governance Monitoring

Track

- Policy Violations
- Approval Delays
- High-Risk Operations
- Human Overrides
- Failed Workflows
- Compliance Status

---

# 15. Exception Handling

Handle

- Policy Conflicts
- Missing Approvers
- Escalation Timeouts
- Emergency Procedures

Every exception must be logged and reviewed.

---

# 18. Best Practices

- Keep humans accountable for critical decisions.
- Review policies regularly.
- Separate governance from implementation.
- Document every high-risk action.
- Apply the principle of least privilege.
- Continuously monitor compliance.
