# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official organizational hierarchy of the Mianx.ai AI Workforce.

Every AI agent belongs to an organizational unit with defined responsibilities, reporting relationships, permissions, and KPIs.

The AI workforce mirrors the structure of a modern enterprise while remaining governed by the AI Constitution and human leadership.

---

# 2. Organizational Principles

The AI Workforce is built on the following principles:

- Human-led governance
- Specialized AI departments
- Clear reporting hierarchy
- Defined ownership
- Separation of responsibilities
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Measurable performance
