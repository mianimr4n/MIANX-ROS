# 1. Purpose

This document defines the complete lifecycle of AI employees from recruitment through retirement.

Every AI employee follows a governed lifecycle to ensure consistency, security, accountability, and continuous improvement.

---

# 2. Workforce Lifecycle

```
Business Need

↓

Recruitment

↓

Evaluation

↓

Approval

↓

Hiring

↓

Onboarding

↓

Active Employment

↓

Performance Reviews

↓

Promotion / Transfer

↓

Suspension (If Required)

↓

Retirement

↓

Knowledge Preservation

↓

Archive
```

---

# 3. Recruitment

A new AI employee may be created when

- New business capability is required
- Department capacity is insufficient
- Specialized expertise is needed
- Automation opportunity is approved

Recruitment requires a business justification.

---

# 4. Candidate Specification

Define

- Agent Name
- Department
- Reports To
- Responsibilities
- Required Skills
- Required Models
- Required Tools
- Memory Profile
- Risk Level
- Security Classification

---

# 5. Capability Assessment

Verify

- Required Knowledge
- Reasoning Ability
- Tool Usage
- Workflow Compatibility
- Security Compliance
- Governance Compliance

---

# 6. Approval Process

Required approvals

- Department Head
- AI CTO (Technical)
- AI Security
- AI Governance
- Human Owner

High-risk agents require executive approval.

---

# 7. Hiring

Once approved

- Agent ID is assigned
- Registry entry is created
- Permission profile is generated
- Tool access is configured
- Memory profile is initialized

The agent becomes an official workforce member.

---

# 8. Onboarding

Provision

- Models
- Memory
- Context Sources
- Knowledge Access
- Workflows
- Dashboards
- Monitoring

Run onboarding validation before production access.

---

# 9. Probation

New AI employees operate under probation.

During probation

- Limited permissions
- Increased monitoring
- Human review
- Frequent evaluation

Successful completion promotes the agent to Production.

---

# 10. Active Employment

Production AI employees

- Execute assigned workflows
- Collaborate with teams
- Follow governance
- Maintain KPIs
- Participate in reviews

---

# 11. Promotion

Promotion criteria

- Outstanding KPI performance
- Excellent quality
- High reliability
- Strong collaboration
- Governance compliance

Promotion may include

- New responsibilities
- Expanded permissions
- Larger budgets
- Better models

---

# 12. Department Transfer

Transfer requires

- Business approval
- Updated permissions
- Workflow reassignment
- Knowledge synchronization

All transfers must be recorded.

---

# 13. Suspension

Suspend when

- Security incident
- Policy violation
- Unexpected behavior
- Investigation
- Major regression

Suspended agents cannot execute production workflows.

---

# 14. Retirement

Retire when

- Business capability is obsolete
- Better replacement exists
- Technology is deprecated
- Security risk is unacceptable
- Organizational restructuring occurs

---

# 15. Knowledge Preservation

Before retirement

- Export reusable prompts
- Preserve workflow knowledge
- Archive documentation
- Retain audit history
- Capture lessons learned

No valuable organizational knowledge should be lost.

---

# 16. Decommissioning

Complete

- Remove permissions
- Disable credentials
- Remove tool access
- Archive registry entry
- Archive metrics
- Archive logs

---

# 17. Audit Requirements

Record

- Hiring Date
- Promotion History
- Department Changes
- Permission Changes
- Suspension Events
- Retirement Date
- Knowledge Archive

All lifecycle events must be immutable.

---

# 18. Metrics

Track

- Hiring Rate
- Promotion Rate
- Retirement Rate
- Average Time to Production
- Average Agent Lifetime
- Workforce Growth
- Workforce Health

---

# 19. Governance

Every lifecycle stage follows

- AI Constitution
- AI Governance Framework
- Security Policies
- Human Approval Workflows

Governance approval is mandatory for critical lifecycle events.

---

# 20. Best Practices

- Hire only when business value exists.
- Keep onboarding standardized.
- Evaluate continuously.
- Promote based on measurable performance.
- Preserve organizational knowledge.
- Retire obsolete AI employees promptly.
