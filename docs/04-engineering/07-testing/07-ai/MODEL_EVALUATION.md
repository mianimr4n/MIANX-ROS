# 1. Purpose

This document defines the enterprise methodology for evaluating, comparing, benchmarking, selecting, and monitoring AI models used within the Telepizza Platform and Mianx.ai.

The objective is to ensure every workload is executed by the most suitable model based on quality, cost, latency, security, governance, and business requirements.

---

# 2. Vision

The best AI model is not the most powerful.

The best AI model is the one that delivers the required business outcome with the optimal balance of

- Accuracy
- Cost
- Speed
- Reliability
- Safety
