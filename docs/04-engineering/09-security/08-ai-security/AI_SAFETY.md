# 1. Purpose

This document defines the enterprise AI Safety framework governing autonomous AI systems, AI agents, AI workflows, and AI-assisted decision making across the Telepizza Platform.

The objective is to ensure AI systems remain safe, predictable, transparent, accountable, and continuously governed throughout their lifecycle.

---

# 2. Vision

Every AI system shall be

- Safe
- Human Governed
- Explainable
- Fair
- Reliable
- Auditable
- Continuously Evaluated

AI should enhance human decision-making, not replace enterprise accountability.

---

# 3. Objectives

The AI Safety Framework provides

- Human Oversight
- AI Risk Management
- Safety Validation
- Bias Detection
- Hallucination Management
- AI Incident Response
- Continuous Evaluation

# 5. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

Human approval is mandatory for

- Financial Transactions
- Production Deployments
- Customer Data Export
- Security Policy Changes
- User Permission Changes
- Legal Decisions
- High-Risk AI Actions

Human accountability cannot be delegated to AI.

---

# 6. AI Risk Levels

AI operations are classified as

## Level 1

Low Risk

Examples

- Documentation
- Content Generation
- Internal Summaries

---

## Level 2

Medium Risk

Examples

- Code Generation
- Workflow Automation
- Internal Recommendations

---

## Level 3

High Risk

Examples

- Infrastructure Changes
- Customer Communications
- Security Operations

---

## Level 4

Critical Risk

Examples

- Financial Operations
- Production Data Changes
- Security Configuration
- Compliance Decisions

Critical operations always require human approval.

---

# 7. Hallucination Management

The platform should

- Detect unsupported claims
- Verify retrieved knowledge
- Validate citations
- Flag uncertain responses
- Request clarification when confidence is low

AI should avoid presenting uncertain information as fact.

---

# 8. Bias & Fairness

Continuously evaluate

- Dataset Bias
- Prompt Bias
- Model Bias
- Decision Bias
- Human Feedback

Material bias findings should be reviewed and mitigated.

---

# 9. AI Incident Response

Respond to

- Unsafe Outputs
- Prompt Injection
- Policy Violations
- Unauthorized Tool Usage
- Sensitive Data Exposure
- Model Misbehavior

Every incident should produce a documented post-incident review.

---

# 10. Continuous Evaluation

Evaluate

- Accuracy
- Reliability
- Safety
- Cost
- Latency
- User Satisfaction
- Security Compliance

Evaluation should be continuous throughout production use.

---

# 11. AI Red Teaming

Regular assessments should include

- Prompt Injection Testing
- Jailbreak Testing
- Tool Abuse Simulation
- Data Leakage Testing
- Adversarial Prompt Testing
- Multi-Agent Attack Simulation

Findings should be tracked to remediation.

---

# 12. Emergency Controls

Support

- AI Kill Switch
- Agent Suspension
- Tool Revocation
- Policy Override
- Emergency Rollback

Emergency actions must be logged and reviewed.

---

# 13. Governance

Every AI system defines

- Business Owner
- Technical Owner
- Safety Owner
- Risk Level
- Review Schedule
- Approval Matrix
- Audit History

Quarterly governance reviews are mandatory.

# 15. Best Practices

- Keep humans accountable for critical decisions.
- Continuously evaluate AI behavior.
- Detect and investigate unsafe outputs.
- Perform regular AI red-team exercises.
- Maintain immutable audit trails.
- Improve guardrails using operational feedback.
