# 1. Purpose

This document defines the enterprise audit framework for applications, infrastructure, cloud services, AI systems, business processes, and security controls across the Telepizza Platform.

The objective is to ensure accountability, transparency, traceability, and continuous assurance.

---

# 2. Vision

Enterprise auditing shall be

- Continuous
- Automated
- Evidence Driven
- AI Assisted
- Tamper Evident
- Fully Traceable

Every critical business and AI action must be auditable.

---

# 3. Objectives

The Audit Framework provides

- Continuous Auditing
- Security Auditing
- AI Auditing
- Compliance Validation
- Evidence Collection
- Executive Reporting
- Operational Transparency

# 5. Audit Scope

Enterprise audits include

- Identity & Access
- Infrastructure
- Applications
- APIs
- Databases
- Networks
- Cloud Resources
- AI Systems
- Business Workflows
- Third-Party Integrations

Every critical asset must be auditable.

---

# 6. Audit Types

Supported audit categories

- Internal Audit
- External Audit
- Security Audit
- Compliance Audit
- AI Governance Audit
- Infrastructure Audit
- Operational Audit
- Vendor Audit

Audit scope should be documented before execution.

---

# 7. AI Audit Framework

Every AI operation records

- Decision ID
- Agent ID
- Model ID
- Prompt ID
- Tool Usage
- Context Sources
- Risk Score
- Policy Evaluation
- Human Approval Status
- Output Validation Result

Every AI decision must be explainable and reproducible where feasible.

---

# 8. Audit Evidence

Evidence includes

- System Logs
- Security Logs
- Configuration Snapshots
- Approval Records
- Deployment History
- Policy Decisions
- AI Execution Logs
- Monitoring Reports
- Vulnerability Reports

Evidence shall be protected against unauthorized modification.

---

# 9. Continuous Auditing

Continuously validate

- Configuration Drift
- Access Changes
- Policy Compliance
- AI Governance
- Security Events
- Infrastructure Changes
- Critical Business Operations

Critical deviations should trigger alerts automatically.

---

# 10. Audit Reporting

Reports should include

- Executive Summary
- Scope
- Findings
- Risk Ratings
- Evidence References
- Recommendations
- Remediation Status

Reports should support executive and technical audiences.

---

# 11. Audit Automation

Automate

- Evidence Collection
- Compliance Checks
- Log Correlation
- Risk Scoring
- AI Decision Review
- Report Generation

Manual effort should focus on analysis rather than data collection.

---

# 12. Audit Retention

Retain

- Audit Logs
- Reports
- Evidence
- AI Decision Records
- Approval History

Retention shall comply with legal, contractual, and regulatory requirements.

---

# 13. Governance

Every audit defines

- Audit Owner
- Audit Scope
- Review Schedule
- Required Evidence
- Approval Authority
- Follow-up Process

Audit governance shall be reviewed annually.

---

# 14. Enterprise KPIs

| KPI | Target |
|------|---------|
| Audit Coverage | 100% |
| Continuous Audit Coverage | 100% |
| Evidence Collection Success | 100% |
| Critical Finding Resolution | ≥95% |
| AI Audit Coverage | 100% |

---

# 15. Best Practices

- Audit continuously instead of periodically.
- Protect audit evidence from tampering.
- Automate repetitive audit activities.
- Maintain immutable audit logs.
- Track remediation until closure.
- Review AI decisions alongside human decisions.
