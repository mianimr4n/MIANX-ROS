# 1. Purpose

This document defines the enterprise compliance framework governing security, privacy, AI systems, infrastructure, software development, and operational processes across the Telepizza Platform.

The framework establishes continuous compliance rather than point-in-time compliance.

---

# 2. Vision

Compliance shall be

- Continuous
- Automated
- Auditable
- Risk-Based
- Evidence Driven
- AI Assisted

Compliance is an ongoing operational capability.

---

# 3. Objectives

The Compliance Framework provides

- Regulatory Compliance
- Security Compliance
- Privacy Compliance
- AI Governance
- Continuous Compliance Monitoring
- Audit Readiness
- Evidence Management

# 5. Supported Frameworks

The platform aligns with

- ISO/IEC 27001
- ISO/IEC 27701
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- SOC 2
- PCI DSS
- OWASP ASVS
- CIS Controls
- GDPR (where applicable)
- Local Privacy Regulations

Additional frameworks may be adopted based on customer or regulatory requirements.

---

# 6. Compliance Domains

Govern

- Identity & Access
- Infrastructure
- Networks
- APIs
- Data Protection
- Cryptography
- AI Systems
- Software Development
- Vendor Management
- Incident Response
- Business Continuity

Every domain shall define measurable controls.

---

# 7. Control Management

Each control defines

- Control ID
- Control Owner
- Business Objective
- Implementation Standard
- Validation Method
- Review Frequency
- Evidence Requirements

Controls must be version controlled.

---

# 8. Continuous Compliance

Continuously monitor

- Configuration Drift
- Security Baselines
- Policy Compliance
- Encryption Coverage
- IAM Compliance
- AI Governance
- Infrastructure Compliance

Non-compliance should generate alerts and remediation tasks.

---

# 9. Evidence Management

Evidence includes

- Audit Logs
- Configuration Snapshots
- Security Reports
- Scan Results
- Approval Records
- Policy Reviews
- AI Decision Logs

Evidence should be retained according to retention policies.

---

# 10. AI Compliance

AI governance includes

- Model Approval
- Prompt Governance
- Human Approval
- Tool Authorization
- AI Risk Classification
- AI Audit Trails

AI systems shall comply with enterprise governance requirements.

- Post-Incident Reviews

- Automate compliance validation.
- Collect evidence continuously.
- Review controls regularly.
- Map controls to business risks.
- Maintain complete audit trails.
- Continuously improve governance.
