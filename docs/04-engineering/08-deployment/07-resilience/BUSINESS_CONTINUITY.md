# 1. Purpose

This document defines the enterprise Business Continuity Framework for the Telepizza Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure critical business operations continue during disruptive events through governance, recovery planning, operational resilience, and continuous improvement.

---

# 2. Vision

Business services should remain

- Available
- Recoverable
- Secure
- Governed
- Resilient
- Continuously Improving

Business continuity extends beyond infrastructure recovery to include people, processes, technology, and suppliers.

---

# 3. Objectives

The Business Continuity Framework provides

- Operational Continuity
- Business Impact Reduction
- Service Prioritization
- Crisis Governance
- Customer Protection
- Regulatory Compliance

# 6. Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Evaluate

- Financial Impact
- Customer Impact
- Operational Impact
- Legal Impact
- Regulatory Impact
- Reputational Impact

BIA should be reviewed annually or after significant business changes.

---

# 7. Continuity Strategies

Support

- High Availability
- Disaster Recovery
- Backup & Restore
- Manual Workarounds
- Alternative Communication
- Cloud Failover

Business strategies should be documented and regularly tested.

---

# 8. Crisis Management

Define

- Incident Commander
- Engineering Lead
- Operations Lead
- Security Lead
- Communications Lead
- Executive Sponsor

Roles and responsibilities must be clearly documented.

---

# 9. Communication Plan

Notify

- Internal Teams
- Executive Leadership
- Customer Support
- Business Partners
- Customers (when required)
- Regulatory Authorities (where applicable)

Use approved communication channels and maintain a single source of truth.

---

# 10. Supplier Continuity

Assess

- Cloud Providers
- Payment Providers
- AI Providers
- SMS Providers
- Email Providers
- Third-Party APIs

Maintain contingency plans for critical external dependencies.

---

# 11. AI Workforce Continuity

Ensure continuity of

- AI Agents
- Model Services
- Prompt Packages
- RAG Services
- Tool Registry
- Memory Services
- AI Governance

Fallback procedures should be documented for AI-dependent workflows.

---

# 12. Continuity Testing

Perform

- Tabletop Exercises
- Recovery Simulations
- Disaster Recovery Drills
- Communication Drills
- Supplier Failure Simulations
- AI Service Recovery Tests

Testing results should be documented and reviewed.

---

# 13. Governance

Every continuity plan defines

- Owner
- Scope
- Review Frequency
- Recovery Objectives
- Testing Schedule
- Approval Authority

Plans should remain current and version controlled.

# 15. Best Practices

- Review continuity plans regularly.
- Keep contact lists current.
- Test recovery procedures.
- Practice crisis communication.
- Identify single points of failure.
- Continuously improve resilience.
