# 🗺️ Telepizza Platform Roadmap

> Official development roadmap for the Telepizza Platform.

---

# 🎯 Vision

Build a modern, AI-powered, multi-branch restaurant platform that manages every aspect of Telepizza Pakistan from one centralized ecosystem.

The platform is designed to support future growth from a single restaurant to a nationwide restaurant chain.

---

# 🏆 Project Goals

- Build a modern customer experience
- Centralize branch management
- Automate restaurant operations
- Improve delivery efficiency
- Increase customer retention
- Support unlimited branches
- Integrate AI into business workflows
- Provide real-time business analytics
